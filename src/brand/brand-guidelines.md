# T-ÉLIXIR Brand Guidelines

## Brand Essence
T-ÉLIXIR is the embodiment of modern luxury perfumery. The brand is refined, confident, and elegantly minimal. Every expression should feel curated, elevated, and timeless.

## Brand Personality
- Luxury
- Elegant
- Timeless
- Minimal
- Premium
- Confident
- Modern
- Powerful
- Sophisticated

## Visual Identity
- Black is the foundation: deep, rich, and grounding.
- Gold is the highlight: subtle illumination and premium artistry.
- Ivory and soft neutrals create spacious balance.
- Clean typography with generous whitespace.
- Minimal compositional layouts.

## Design Principles
1. Create calm and sophisticated scenes.
2. Use dramatic negative space.
3. Keep elements restrained and purposeful.
4. Favor visual hierarchy over decoration.
5. Let premium materials and typography speak.

## Logo and Wordmark
- Use the T-ÉLIXIR name with understated elegance.
- Avoid loud ornamentation.
- Maintain clean spacing around the mark.

## Photography & Imagery
- Use images that feel editorial and cinematic.
- Avoid noisy or overly saturated visuals.
- Favor texture, shadow, and luxury lighting.

## Tone and Voice
- Refined, simple, and confident.
- Calm, assured, and never aggressive.
- Never cheap or overly casual.
- Each statement should feel selective and considered.
