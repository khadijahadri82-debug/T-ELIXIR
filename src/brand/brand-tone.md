# T-ÉLIXIR Brand Tone

## Core Voice Attributes
- Luxury: Every sentence should feel valuable and deliberate.
- Simple: Clarity is essential. Use words that are precise and elegant.
- Confident: Speak with quiet assurance.
- Never aggressive.
- Never cheap.
- Never noisy.

## Tone Guidelines
- Use gently assertive language.
- Prefer calm statements over exuberance.
- Avoid superlatives unless they are true and subtle.
- Keep copy concise, polished, and evocative.

## Word Selection
- Use refined words: crafted, scent, residence, ritual, luminous.
- Avoid trendy or overly casual language.
- Use sensory cues to evoke mood, not literal lists.

## Sentence Style
- Short to medium length.
- Focus on emotion and atmosphere.
- Use active voice with understated confidence.
- Avoid excess punctuation and exclamation marks.
