# T-ÉLIXIR Copywriting Rules

## Buttons
- Use inviting, action-led labels.
- Keep copy short and elegant.
- Avoid commands that feel forceful.
- Examples: Explore, Discover, Reserve, View Details.

## Titles
- Keep titles clear and poetic.
- Use simple, evocative phrases.
- Prioritize brand clarity over complexity.

## Headlines
- Create premium tension with brevity.
- Evoke feeling, not explanation.
- Use strong nouns and refined adjectives.

## Subtitles
- Provide context in a calm tone.
- Use slightly softer language than headlines.
- Maintain readability and spacing.

## Descriptions
- Keep descriptive copy minimal and sensory.
- Focus on atmosphere, material, and ritual.
- Avoid long paragraphs.

## Product Text
- Highlight craftsmanship, scent personality, and experience.
- Use precise language for ingredients and emotion.
- Do not over-explain.

## Marketing Text
- Speak to aspiration with understated confidence.
- Emphasize quality and rarity.
- Use storytelling cues without embellishment.

## CTA Rules
- Invite gently.
- Use verbs that feel curated.
- Avoid hard-sell language.
- Keep calls-to-action calm and direct.
